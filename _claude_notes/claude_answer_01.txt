Three design decisions in there are the real assets, and each is the kind of thing that is normally got wrong and discovered late:

1. wiring.json stores designators, not coordinates. That's what lets connectivity survive re-extraction, a second sheet, and a renumbering of every C####.
2. Authored geometry is stored as polylines with conductors as provenance only. Which is why the layer-0 extraction bug I found is annoying rather than catastrophic — nothing you've authored dies.
3. The loop is already closed, one click wide. I had assumed it wasn't. It is: prompts.py makes the model spell every identifier as CR-BP:A1, Markdown.tsx renders those as Citation, and a click calls select(kind, id) and switches to the Drawing tab, which paints it. The model already answers graphically — just one identifier per click, driven by your finger.

A rewrite throws those three away and you would rediscover them over months. The question isn't whether to keep this; it's what to stop doing.
